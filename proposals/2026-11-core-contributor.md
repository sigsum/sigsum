# Proposal on adding core contributors

## Background

It would be nice to give more credit to the people contributing to Sigsum.
Other than being a nice thing to do, something as little as having your name
listed on the Sigsum website could help in practise when e.g. applying for
funding.  "Aha, this is a person that's actually associated with the project".

Many projects eventually introduce core contributors.  Such core contributors
usually have "higher status" in the project, and might get perks like being
invited to community meetings, being able to vote on certain project things,
being listed on a website, etc.  See for example [Tor's core member process][].

[Tor's core member process]: https://community.torproject.org/policies/membership/

rgdd and a few others have been thinking about introducing core contributors for
a while, but it's fizzled out because someone needs to define it (and perfect
becomes the enemy of good; as mentioned above, just a list of names is good).

The intent for this proposal is to do something in the direction of having core
contributors, but to keep it simple and mostly just "list names on our website".

## Proposal

Add a subsection on <https://www.sigsum.org/about> named "Core contributors".
This subsection lists all the core contributors (one bullet per contributor).

Concrete suggestion:

```
## Core contributors

People that actively contribute to the project and engage with each other are
listed here.

  - Name 1
  - [Name 2](https://www.example.org/)
  - Name 3 (nickname)
```

### Becoming a core contributor

Actively contribute to Sigsum, and actively engage with the existing core
contributors.  "Actively" refers to the past 6-12 months (rule of thumb).

After earning the respect of the existing core contributors, ask one of them to
sponsor you by queueing up a decision to add you on a weekly Sigsum meeting.

### Responsibilities of being a core contributor

Continue to contribute to the project and engage with other core contributors.

Use your good judgement to be a good ambassador of the project.

### Benefits of being a core contributor

Your name is listed on the Sigsum project's website.

### Stop being a core contributor

Similar to membership: queue this up for decision on a weekly meet.

  - Voluntary: it is requested
  - Inactivity: there's little or no contribution for a long time

## Discussion

We could probably think of more things that could go into this proposal.  The
goal for this proposal is to keep as much of those 'extras' out of scope.

For now there is no intent to document this "core contributor" status further
than just adding the list of names on the Sigsum website.  The actual list of
names is also intentionally left out of this proposal, to not block on that.  We
expect that the list will be mainly be composed of Sigsum weekly regulars.
